Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

PromptVault is a free, private, non-commercial hobby project. It is a single-page web app for organizing text prompts. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: everything stays in your own browser, and the app does not send any of your data to a server.

Who is responsible

This app is operated privately by an individual as a hobby project, with no commercial purpose. You can reach the operator at:

tastyteabots@proton.me

No accounts, no tracking

  • There is no registration, login, or user account.
  • There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no cookies set by the app.
  • The app does not create any usage profiles and does not collect personal data on a server.

No external resources

The entire app is contained in a single HTML file. It does not load any external fonts, scripts, libraries, CDNs, analytics, or tracking services. No data is transmitted to third parties while you use the app.

  • Fonts: only fonts already installed on your device are used (e.g. Segoe UI, Consolas) — no web fonts are downloaded.
  • Styles and scripts: fully embedded in the page — no external files are requested.
  • No requests (e.g. fetch) are made to any server while using the app.

Data stored in your browser (localStorage)

The data you create is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage on your own device. It never leaves your browser and is not accessible to the operator. The following entries are used:

KeyContents
promptvault_dataYour categories and the prompts within them.
promptvault_data_backupAn automatic backup copy of the previous version of your data.
promptvault_favsYour favorite prompts, grouped by category.
promptvault_templatesTemplates you save in the Builder.
This data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data / localStorage for this page, or by using your browser's settings. Using the app in a private/incognito window means the data is removed when you close that window.

The Import and Export functions read and write a JSON file locally on your device only. Exporting or importing does not send anything over the network.

Hosting and server logs (GitHub Pages)

This app is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. (USA), a subsidiary of Microsoft. When you open the page, GitHub's servers automatically process technical connection data in their server logs, including your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested file, and your browser's user agent. This is technically necessary to deliver the page and is outside the operator's control.

Because GitHub is based in the USA, this can involve a transfer of personal data to a third country. Details on how GitHub processes this data are available in GitHub's privacy statement:

GitHub General Privacy Statement

Legal basis

Where the GDPR applies, the processing of connection data for delivering the page is based on the legitimate interest in providing the website in a technically secure and functional way (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The app itself does not process personal data on a server.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Since the app itself stores your data only locally in your browser and the operator has no access to it, the operator cannot fulfill these requests for that local data — you remain in full control of it on your own device. For requests concerning server-side log data, please contact GitHub via the link above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) if you believe the processing of your personal data infringes the GDPR.
